Problem & Solution (Crisis vs Solution)

Traditional Manual Mandi Pitfalls vs. Harvest Hub Digital Solution

Eliminating middlemen, excessive chemical degradation, unscientific moisture cuts, and traffic gridlocks across Indian agricultural markets.

⚠️

Traditional Manual APMC System

Manual Paper Mandi Challenges
  • ✗

    Indiscriminate Chemical Use & Soil Degradation

    Over 78% of farmers overuse urea & DAP without testing, causing soil organic carbon to drop dangerously below 0.4%.

  • ✗

    Unscientific Quality Cuts & Zero Incentive

    Arbitrary deductions are made for moisture and foreign matter with zero reward for farmers practicing soil conservation.

  • ✗

    24 to 48 Hour Mandi Traffic Gridlocks

    Hundreds of tractor-trolleys line highways in scorching heat and rain, causing crop spoilage and heavy freight losses.

  • ✗

    Handwritten Paper Slips & Payment Delays

    Manual weighment slips are prone to manipulation, and bank settlements take weeks through intermediaries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key Questions About Harvest Hub

Answers to common inquiries from farmers, procurement officers, and researchers.

1. How does the 48-Hour Scientific Advisory SLA work? ▼
When a farmer requests pre-sowing soil testing, the lab team collects soil samples and records NPK & pH parameters. An automated 48-working-hour countdown timer is activated. The agricultural procurement officer must formulate and issue a certified seed variety and fertilizer prescription (Urea/DAP/Zinc) within 48 working hours.
2. Why was post-harvest laboratory testing eliminated? ▼
In traditional systems, post-harvest lab testing causes days of delay, and farmers face arbitrary cuts for moisture or grade. In Harvest Hub, quality is established pre-sowing through soil testing and certified compliance. Post-harvest lot registration is immediate, locking in guaranteed Base MSP + Soil Compliance Bonus without delays.
3. What is the Soil Compliance Bonus? ▼
Farmers who 100% adhere to the prescribed fertilizer and seed advisories earn a 'Certified Compliance' badge. When delivering their harvest to the mandi, they receive a direct cash bonus of ₹50 to ₹115 per quintal added on top of the government Minimum Support Price (MSP), paid straight to their bank account via DBT.
4. Can multiple crops be sold in a single vehicle? ▼
Yes! Harvest Hub supports multi-crop token bookings. If a farmer has harvested both wheat and mustard from different parcels, both crops can be selected in a single gate pass and transported in the same tractor-trolley. Sequential gross-tare weighments generate a unified transparent digital bill.
5. Does this platform work with poor rural internet connectivity? ▼
Yes. Harvest Hub is engineered to offline-first web standards and loads rapidly on rural 2G/3G connections. Furthermore, the 'Kisan Vani' voice assistant enables farmers to inquire about mandi rates, weather, and advisories simply by speaking into their phones without typing.
