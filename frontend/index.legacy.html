💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Operational FAQ & Help

Clear operational answers to common questions about slot booking, digital tokens, and procurement.

Farm Route is a digital agricultural procurement and queue management platform. It allows farmers to reserve arrival time slots at government-authorized centers, receive cryptographic digital tokens, and track real-time queue status directly from their mobile phones.
No, Farm Route is entirely free for all farmers. There are no fees for searching centers, reserving time slots, tracking queue positions, or downloading digital procurement receipts.
Navigate to 'Procurement Centers', choose your crop (e.g., Wheat, Mustard, Paddy) and district. The dynamic throughput engine highlights low-congestion alternative centers with minimal wait times.
When you book a slot, you receive an HMAC-signed digital pass with an ID (#KS-TKN-xxxx) and QR code. Center operators scan this QR code at the entrance gate to verify your arrival and grant priority lane entry.
The system provides a grace buffer. If delayed on the road, you can report a transit delay (+60 min window) from your portal. Vehicles arriving past their buffer are accommodated via the Standby Lane without disrupting scheduled arrivals.
In the 'Live Queue' section of the Farmer Portal, you can see your vehicle's exact place in line, the count of vehicles ahead, the active weighbridge scale, and estimated wait minutes.
Yes. As long as your booking is in CONFIRMED status and you haven't yet checked in at the entry gate, you can cancel or rebook at any time from your Farmer Dashboard.
Upon gate entry, the vehicle undergoes moisture testing at the assay lab. It is then weighed on an electronic weighbridge (Gross Loaded Weight). After grain unloading at the storage shed, the empty trolley is weighed (Tare Weight) to calculate exact net grain tonnage.
As soon as tare weighing is verified by the operator, an official statutory APMC Form 'J' e-receipt (#KSP-RCP-xxxx) is generated instantly. You can view, download, or print it from your portal, and payment is processed via DBT.
This platform is developed as a production-grade working prototype and proof-of-concept for Smart India Hackathon (SIH). It operates with realistic simulated data and API connectivity to demonstrate the complete end-to-end digital market workflow.
