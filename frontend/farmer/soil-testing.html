Critical Advisory: When NOT to Use Fertilizers

Critical Safety Rules

Applying fertilizers at the wrong time or improper rate not only wastes input investment, but also causes severe crop damage, lodging, and pest outbreaks:

  • ❌ Do NOT apply Urea after flowering or heading: Causes vegetative surge and lodging, hinders grain filling, and increases fungal susceptibility.
  • ❌ Never mix DAP directly with Zinc Sulphate: Forms insoluble Zinc Phosphate, rendering both fertilizers unavailable to crops.
  • ❌ Do NOT broadcast Urea on dry soil or in strong midday sun: Escapes as ammonia gas via volatilization and can scorch plant foliage.
  • ❌ Do NOT apply Nitrogen before heavy rainfall: Fertilizer leaches away with surface runoff, resulting in complete wastage.

How to Collect Soil Sample Accurately?

1. Dig 'V'-Shaped Cuts:

Dig 15 cm (6 inches) deep 'V'-shaped slices across 5 zigzag spots in your plot.

2. Clear Surface Litter:

Do not sample under trees, near farm bunds, or close to manure heaps.

3. Mix & Shade Dry:

Combine all 5 subsamples on a clean tarp, shade-dry, and pack approx. 500 grams into a clean bag.

4. Attach Token Label:

Label with your name, mobile, and sample token ID, then present it at the lab.
