STRICT RESTRICTIONS: When NOT to Use Fertilizers

Applying chemical fertilizers in these conditions damages crops and causes financial loss:
🌧️ 1. Forecast of Heavy Rainfall or Storm (Rainfall & Moisture)

If heavy rain or waterlogging is expected within 24 to 48 hours, never apply Urea or fertilizers. Leaching & runoff will waste up to 70% of nitrogen and contaminate local groundwater.

🧪 2. Never Mix Zinc Sulphate with DAP or Phosphatic Fertilizers

Warning: Never mix DAP or SSP with Zinc Sulphate. They react to form insoluble Zinc Phosphate that plants cannot absorb. Maintain at least 4-5 days gap between applications.

🌾 3. Peak Flowering & Grain Filling Stage

Never apply urea top-dressing after flowering or heading stage. Excess nitrogen triggers uncontrolled vegetative growth, shrinks grain size, and induces crop lodging.

🫘 4. Urea Top-Dressing in Pulses & Legumes (Legume Warning)

Legume roots host Rhizobium nodules that naturally fix atmospheric nitrogen. Top-dressing urea destroys nodules, causes excessive foliage, and hinders pod formation.

☀️ 5. Severe Dry Soil & Direct Noon Heat

Applying urea on dry soil causes fertilizer burn and salt injury to root tips. Midday heat volatilizes nitrogen into ammonia gas. Always apply fertilizer in late evening with adequate moisture.
